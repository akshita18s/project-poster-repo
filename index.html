The Toolkit

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Six tools that cover the moments that matter most, built to work when the signal doesn't.

One-tap SOS

A single hold fires your live location to emergency contacts and the nearest response desk — no unlocking, no typing, no signal required.

Offline emergency helplines

National and state helpline numbers cached on your device, searchable and dialable in airplane mode.

Live disaster alerts

Flood, landslide, cyclone and heatwave warnings across 12 states, pushed the moment they're issued.

Nearest police & hospital

Ranked by real travel time, not straight-line distance — with hours, phone numbers and directions.

9-language voice translator

Speak and be understood in nine Indian languages — the phrasebook works offline too.

Safety Pass

Verification that unlocks the app locally. Your identity is checked on-device and never uploaded — privacy-first by design.
